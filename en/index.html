More about me

Before your first visit

Where are sessions held?

In Tbilisi, Mtatsminda district, near Liberty Square metro. I'll send the exact address after you book.

What should I bring?

Just a hair tie. The session is done in your underwear; towels are provided. I don't use disposable sheets — you'll have your own cotton sheet.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer, after the session. The Intro Session is prepaid.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — message me on Telegram (@vanmaugli) or the bot. More than 24 hours before the session — free; 24 to 12 hours — 50% of the price; later — 100%.

Any contraindications?

Yes: fever, acute inflammation, infections in the acute phase and a few other conditions. The full list is on the services page.

How soon after giving birth?

From 1–2 weeks after birth; after a C-section — once the incision has healed. A doctor's approval is required. The session is without the baby.
